name: research-grill
description: "Clarify and challenge a research idea before committing to experiments."
---

## Inputs

The idea, existing records, constraints and decision owner.

## Procedure

1. Read the project first. Look up facts yourself; ask the researcher for decisions.
2. Separate the business objective from the scientific question. Name the population, comparison and outcome.
3. List assumptions. Label each supported, assumed or unknown; link evidence.
4. Challenge the assumption most likely to invalidate the project. Ask only questions whose prerequisites are settled; recommend an answer and explain the tradeoff.
5. Agree on a baseline, minimum useful effect, budget, non-goals and stop rule.
6. Stop interviewing when the next experiment can answer the remaining uncertainty. Record unresolved decisions and owners.

## Outputs and evidence

brief.md and project.json: objective, question, assumptions, success criterion and next step.

## Completion criteria

The next experiment is clear; product choices and scientific claims are separate.

## Resources

Use the [output template](assets/output.md). Consult the [record contract](references/record-contract.md) for IDs, paths and evidence rules. Keep work local/offline unless another tool is explicitly selected. Never fabricate evidence; preserve failures and separate observation from interpretation.
