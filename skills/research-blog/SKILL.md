# Research Blog

## Inputs

Approved research summary, source artifacts/figures, audience, disclosure constraints and evidence ledger.

## Procedure

1. Choose a reader question and explain why it matters. State scope and the scientific question before implementation details.
2. Explain the baseline, method and experimental controls with enough detail to understand the comparison. Link the full reproduction record.
3. Present results by question, not chronological run order. Include figures with captions, uncertainty, failures and evidence IDs.
4. Distinguish measured outcomes from interpretation and future ideas. Do not strengthen claims beyond the summary or hide inconvenient runs.
5. Describe practical implications and limitations, including data coverage, leakage controls, compute and transfer assumptions.
6. Verify every numerical statement against a source table and every citation against its actual content. Re-render/inspect figures after editing.
7. Save as an internal draft with references and next steps. Publication or messaging is a separate user decision; the skill's deliverable is the article.

## Outputs and evidence

blog.md with linked figures, evidence ledger, reproduction pointer and explicit draft status.

Use stable project/hypothesis/experiment/run IDs and relative evidence paths. Preserve failed attempts. Never fabricate missing measurements, citations or verification. Keep observed facts separate from interpretation.

## Completion criteria

The article is readable without the run log, numbers are source-checked, uncertainty remains intact and no publication is implied.
