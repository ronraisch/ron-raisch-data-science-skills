# Contributing

Prefer a focused improvement over another broad catalog entry. Describe a realistic trigger, required inputs, local/offline procedure, concrete deliverables and completion criteria. Add a fixture showing the skill helps detect or resolve a research mistake. Do not require a commercial model or service.

For supporting code, add tests for the scientific failure being prevented: leakage, unintended trainability, wrong checkpoint/settings, missing evidence or confounded comparison. Keep negative outcomes visible. Run the unittest suite and CPU demo. New verifier adapters must document what they establish and what they cannot.

Upstream adaptations require a pinned source, per-file license review, attribution and retained notices. Do not silently relabel source-available services as open source. Avoid model-generated charts that could imply fabricated evidence.
