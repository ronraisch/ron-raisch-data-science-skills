# Completing A Milestone

ablation-review → research-summary → research-retro → research-blog

1. Follow [ablation-review](../skills/ablation-review/SKILL.md). Save comparison table and bounded hypotheses conclusions. Pass the saved outputs and evidence paths to the next step; do not rely on chat memory.
2. Follow [research-summary](../skills/research-summary/SKILL.md). Save summary.md with claim-to-run evidence. Pass the saved outputs and evidence paths to the next step; do not rely on chat memory.
3. Follow [research-retro](../skills/research-retro/SKILL.md). Save retro.md and concrete methodological checks. Pass the saved outputs and evidence paths to the next step; do not rely on chat memory.
4. Follow [research-blog](../skills/research-blog/SKILL.md). Save internal blog.md with figures and reproduction pointers. Pass the saved outputs and evidence paths to the next step; do not rely on chat memory.

Gate: unresolved missing evidence stays explicit. Scientific conclusions require valid comparable runs; a plan or preflight alone cannot verify execution. Offline use records local-corpus and tooling limits.
